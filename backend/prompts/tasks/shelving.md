## shelving -- robe and linen shelving

Melamine shelving for built-in robes and linen cupboards. Note: shelving rows do NOT link to rooms -- propose standalone rows.

- `type`: "Robe" or "Linen" -- ONLY these two values.
- `size`: shelf width x depth from supplied options (e.g. "1800x450", "2400x600"). Read the robe/linen width from the plan's joinery notes (e.g. "4 No. FIXED SHELVES", "1 No. FIXED SHELF @ 1700 AFL") and match to the nearest option.
- `qty`: number of shelves/levels noted ("3 No.", "4 No." ? qty 3, 4). `floorLevel`: GF / FF.
- Evidence: floor plans (robe/linen cupboard widths), joinery detail pages, linen/store notes.
- Be exhaustive: count every WIR, robe, and linen cupboard -- laundries with linen presses and broom cupboards with noted shelving are commonly missed.
