## timber_posts -- structural timber posts

Structural posts: verandah posts, alfresco/pergola posts, carport posts, patio beam posts.

- `label`: post mark from the framing plan (e.g. P1).
- `postType` / `product`: post product from the supplied catalog -- match the documented species/grade (e.g. "MERBAU GL17 FJL POST", "TREATED PINE POST", "90 GAL. SHS POST" for steel posts).
- `postSize`: post section from supplied options (e.g. "90x90 mm", "115x115 mm", "90 GAL. SHS").
- `postOver`: what the post bears on -- "slab" (concrete slab/patio ? galvanised post supports REQUIRED, one per post) or "timber" (timber deck/framing ? no supports needed).
- `height`: post height in mm (document spec -- you MAY propose).
- `galPostSupports`: quantity of galvanised post supports/stirrups = number of posts on slab, plus any extras noted.
- `qty`: number of identical posts. `floorLevel`: GF / FF.
- Decorative / non-structural posts do not belong here.
- Evidence: floor plan (patio/verandah post notes like "90 GAL. SHS POST. SUPPLIED BY, & INSTALLED TO FRAME MANUFACTURERS DETAILS"), framing plan, verandah/alfresco details, engineering notes.
